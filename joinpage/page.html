Cemu

On a computer, with the Cemu emulator and your own copy of the game.

{{if eq .ErrorFor "cemu"}}

{{.Error}}

{{end}}
{{if .NeedsInvite}} {{end}}
  1. Start Cemu once, then close it.
  2. In Cemu, File > Open Cemu folder, and copy everything from the zip into it, merging folders.
  3. In Options > General settings > Account, pick your username, tick Enable online mode, and set the network service to Custom.
  4. Start the game and go into Course World.

Wii U

On a Wii U running Aroma that already plays on Pretendo. Only Super Mario Maker comes here; everything else stays on Pretendo.

{{if eq .ErrorFor "wiiu"}}

{{.Error}}

{{end}}
{{if .NeedsInvite}} {{end}}
  1. Copy the wiiu folder from the zip onto the root of your SD card.
  2. Start the console, sign in to that Pretendo account, and start Super Mario Maker.
  3. To leave, delete smm-private-server.wps from the SD card.
{{end}}

Good to know

Your zip lets whoever has it play as you. Keep it to yourself.

In Cemu, makers show without a face: Cemu can't draw Mii faces without files from a real Wii U.
